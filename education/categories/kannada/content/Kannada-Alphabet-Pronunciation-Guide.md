# ಅಕ್ಷರ ಉಚ್ಚಾರಣೆ ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ (Alphabet Pronunciation Guide)

Kannada Alphabet Pronunciation Guide is a helpful worksheet designed for children and beginners to learn Kannada letters correctly. This guide includes Kannada vowels and consonants, along with their English pronunciations, making it easy for learners to understand and practice the correct sounds.

The guide helps children recognize letters, improve pronunciation, and build strong reading skills. It is especially useful for early learners, young children, and anyone who is new to the Kannada language.

This guide supports confident learning by making Kannada alphabet pronunciation simple, clear, and enjoyable..
