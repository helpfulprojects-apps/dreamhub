# Colors in Kannada

Make learning fun and cultural with this Colors in Kannada resource! Designed for young learners, this engaging material introduces children to basic colors in Kannada, helping them expand their vocabulary while connecting language with visuals.

What’s Inside:
Common Colors in Kannada: Learn color names like ಕೆಂಪು (Kempu – Red), ಹಸಿರು (Hasiru – Green), ನೀಲಿ (Neeli – Blue), ಬಿಳಿ (Bili – White), and more.

Color-Word Matching Activities: Match the Kannada word to the correct color

Color and Learn Pages: Fun coloring activities that reinforce learning.

Skills Practiced:
✔ Kannada reading and writing
✔ Color recognition
✔ Vocabulary development
✔ Fine motor skills through tracing and coloring
