# Brain Teaser: Find the Different Number – Fun Critical Thinking Activity


Challenge your child’s observation and problem-solving skills with this exciting Find the Different Number brain teaser! In each set, one or two numbers are slightly different from the rest — and it’s your job to spot it.

## What It Builds:
🔍 Attention to detail

🧠 Critical thinking

🤔 Logical reasoning

👁️‍🗨️ Visual discrimination

Perfect for everyone, this activity makes learning fun while strengthening brain power. Great for warm-ups, early finishers, and screen-free brain breaks!
