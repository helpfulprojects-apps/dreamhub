# Counting Worksheets – Fun and Interactive Math Practice for Early Learners

## Description:

Make learning to count fun and engaging with these adorable and easy-to-follow Counting Worksheets! Designed especially for Preschool, Pre-K, and Kindergarten students, this resource helps children develop essential early math skills through hands-on activities and visual learning.

## What’s Inside:
Count and Write:
Count objects like balls, balloons, and everyday items, then write the correct number in the box.

Circle the Correct Number:
Multiple-choice exercises where children count the objects and circle the correct number.

Count and Match:
Match the number of objects to the correct numeral, enhancing number recognition and one-to-one correspondence.

Chart Practice:
Count groups of objects and record answers in a simple chart format to build organization and early data skills.

## Skills Practiced:
✔ Counting from 1 to 10 (and beyond)
✔ Number recognition
✔ One-to-one correspondence
✔ Fine motor skills through writing and circling

Perfect for classroom centers, homeschooling, or extra at-home practice, these worksheets are an excellent way to build your child’s math confidence from the very beginning.
