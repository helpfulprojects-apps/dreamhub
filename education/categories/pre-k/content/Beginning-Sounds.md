# Beginning Sounds Worksheets for Pre-K and Kindergarten

Beginning sounds help kids develop early literacy skills, specifically focusing on recognizing and identifying the initial sounds of words.

Alphabet Recognition
Phonemic Awareness
Vocabulary Building
Pre-Reading Skills
Letter-Sound Correspondence
Visual Discrimination
Listening Skills
Cognitive Skills

Overall, learning beginning sounds provides an interactive and enjoyable way for children to start understanding the connection between letters and the sounds they make, setting the stage for successful reading development.
