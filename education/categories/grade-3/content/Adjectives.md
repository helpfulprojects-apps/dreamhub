## What’s Inside:
What is an Adjective?
A simple explanation: adjectives are words that describe nouns (people, places, animals, or things).

Examples of Adjectives:

Describing how something looks: tall, round, colorful
Describing feelings: happy, excited, sleepy
Describing size, shape, number, texture, and more

How Adjectives Help:
They make writing more interesting by giving more detail—so instead of “a dog,” you can say “a fluffy, brown dog.”

Types of Adjectives Covered:

Descriptive Adjectives (e.g., shiny, rough)
Quantity Adjectives (e.g., few, many)
Comparative & Superlative Adjectives (e.g., bigger, biggest)
