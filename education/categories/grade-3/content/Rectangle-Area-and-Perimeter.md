# Rectangle Area and Perimeter – Complete Learning Guide with Worksheets

## Description:
Make math meaningful with this interactive and easy-to-understand Rectangle Area and Perimeter learning resource! ## What’s Inside:
Understanding Rectangles:

Learn the properties of rectangles – 4 sides, 4 right angles, equal opposite sides, and more.

Explore how length and width define the shape.

Key Concepts Explained:

Area: The amount of space inside a rectangle, calculated as Length × Width.

Perimeter: The distance around a rectangle, calculated as 2 × (Length + Width).

Visual grids and labeled diagrams to aid comprehension.

## Practice Worksheets Include:

Basic Calculations: Find the area and perimeter of rectangles.

Find Missing Sides: Solve problems to find length or width using given area or perimeter.

Word Problems: Apply knowledge to real-life situations like gardens, pools, whiteboards, and parks.

Answer Keys Provided for self-checking or guided review.
