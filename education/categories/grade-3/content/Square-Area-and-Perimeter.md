## What’s Included:
Introduction to Squares:

Learn about square properties: equal sides, right angles, and symmetrical diagonals.

Area and Perimeter Made Simple:

Area = Side × Side (measured in square units)

Perimeter = 4 × Side (measured in units)

Grid-based visual explanations for better understanding.
