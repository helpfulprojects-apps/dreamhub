# Christmas Reading Comprehension for Grade 1
 Read the paragraph and answer the question below: 

Christmas is a special time of the year when people celebrate with their families and friends. It happens every year on December 25th. Many people decorate their homes with lights, Christmas trees, and shiny ornaments. Children love Christmas because they get gifts from Santa Claus, who comes in a big red suit and rides a sleigh pulled by reindeer. Families enjoy singing Christmas songs, eating yummy treats like cookies, and spending time together. Some people also give gifts to others to show kindness and love. Christmas is a happy time filled with joy and laughter.


## Questions:

1. When is Christmas celebrated every year?


2. What do people use to decorate their homes at Christmas?


3. Who brings gifts to children on Christmas?


4. What treats do families enjoy during Christmas?


5. Why do people give gifts to others during Christmas?
