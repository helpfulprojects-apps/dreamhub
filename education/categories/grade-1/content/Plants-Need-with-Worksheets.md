# What Plants Need – A Educational Guide with Worksheets (Grade 1)

## Description:
Introduce your young learner to the basics of plant care and growth with this colorful and interactive “Plants Needs” resource! Designed for Grade 1, this guide explains what plants need to survive in simple, kid-friendly language and includes fun facts, engaging visuals, and hands-on worksheets to reinforce learning.

## What’s Inside:
Key Concepts Covered:
    Sunlight: Helps plants make their own food
    Water: Keeps them fresh and upright
    Air: Plants breathe in carbon dioxide and give out oxygen
    Soil: Provides support and nutrients
    Space: Gives room for roots and leaves to grow
Fun Facts & Real-Life Connections:
    Learn how plants clean our air and why giving them space is important
    Encourages kids to observe soil and plant growth at home

## Worksheets Included:
Matching Game: Match plant needs to pictures
Fill in the Blanks: Reinforce vocabulary and comprehension
Circle the Right Picture: Identify what helps plants grow
True or False: Test basic understanding of plant needs
Draw & Color Activity: Let kids create their own healthy plant scene

✔ Enhances vocabulary and observation skills
✔ Encourages creativity and hands-on learning
