# Basics about plants - A Simple Guide about Plants for Kids

This small PDF introduces young learners to the fascinating world of plants in an easy-to-understand way. Perfect for kids, parents, and teachers, it covers the basics of plant life with engaging visuals and simple language.

## What's Inside:

Parts of a Plant: Learn about roots, stems, leaves, flowers, and their functions.
How Plants Grow: Explore the life cycle of a plant from seed to maturity.
What Plants Need: Understand the essentials like sunlight, water, air, and soil.


Interactive Activities: Includes simple questions and tasks to reinforce learning.
This PDF is ideal for kids in Kindergarten, and Grade1 providing a foundation in plant science while sparking curiosity about nature.

Feel free to download this whenever you want to teach about plants to your kids.
