# Addition Worksheets For kids 3 to 6 years old

Hello everyone!

I’ve created a collection of addition worksheets designed for kids aged 3 to 6. These worksheets are a fun and engaging way to introduce little learners to basic math concepts. The set includes single-digit addition problems, along with a few two-digit addition challenges for kids ready to take the next step.

Feel free to download the worksheets, print them out, and let your child explore and solve them at their own pace. It’s a great way to help your little one practice math while building confidence in their skills.

Enjoy this resource, and happy learning!
