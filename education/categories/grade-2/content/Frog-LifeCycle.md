# Frog Life Cycle – Educational Content & Worksheets for Kids
Hop into the fascinating world of amphibians with this engaging Frog Life Cycle resource! Perfect for young learners, this content explains each stage of a frog’s development with simple language, colorful visuals, and interactive worksheets that reinforce understanding in a fun way.


## What's Inside:
Detailed Life Cycle Explanation:

    Egg Stage: Learn how frogspawn develops in water.
    Tadpole Stage: Discover how tadpoles hatch and live underwater with gills.
    Froglet Stage: See how legs grow, tails shrink, and lungs form.
    Adult Frog: Understand how frogs adapt to live on land and in water.

## What is Metamorphosis?
A simple explanation of the transformation from tadpole to frog.
