# Months of the Year Worksheets
Help your child master the Months of the Year with these interactive and educational worksheets! Designed to make learning fun, this resource introduces young learners to the calendar while improving their reading and sequencing skills.

## What's Inside:
Introduction to Months: Learn all 12 months in order, along with their spellings and key characteristics.
Fun Activities: Sequencing tasks to reinforce understanding.
Months Recognition: Activities to identify and name all 12 moths of the year.
Sequencing Practice: Fun exercises to arrange the months in the correct order.
Spelling Activities: Trace and write each month to build early literacy skills.
Interactive Drawing Fun: Drawing your imagination for each day. 

## Benefits:
Builds foundational calendar skills.
Enhances memory and sequencing abilities.
Encourages creativity and hands-on learning.


Perfect for children in Preschool through 2nd grade, these worksheets are great for at-home practice or classroom use. Feel free to download and let your child enjoy exploring the months of the year! 🗓️
