# Sugarcane Juice without Sugarcane

This easy homemade drink tastes surprisingly close to fresh sugarcane juice—made using jaggery, ginger, lemon, and mint. It’s refreshing and  naturally sweet.
![Sugarcane Juice](images/Sugarcane2.jpg){md}

## 🕒 Prep Time

5–7 minutes


## 🍹 Servings

1 glass

## Ingredients
Jaggery – 2 tablespoons (grated or powdered)
Fresh ginger – 1 inch piece (peeled and chopped)
Lemon juice – 1 tablespoon (freshly squeezed)
Fresh mint leaves – 6–8 leaves
Water – 1 to 1½ cups (adjust as needed)

## Steps
1. Prepare the ingredients
Peel and roughly chop the ginger. Wash the mint leaves thoroughly. If the jaggery is in solid form, grate or break it into small pieces so it blends easily.
![Sugarcane Juice](images/Sugarcane4.jpg){md}

2. Add to blender
Add the jaggery, chopped ginger, mint leaves, and lemon juice into a blender jar.
![Sugarcane Juice](images/Sugarcane.jpg){md}

3. Add water
Pour in 1 to 1½ cups of water, depending on how strong or light you want the drink.
![Sugarcane Juice](images/Sugarcane6.jpg){md}

4. Blend well
Blend everything on high speed for 30–45 seconds until the jaggery is fully dissolved and the mixture looks smooth.
![Sugarcane Juice](images/Sugarcane5.jpg){md}

5. Strain the juice
Using a fine strainer, strain the blended mixture into a glass to remove ginger and mint residue.
![Sugarcane Juice](images/Sugarcane3.jpg){md}

6. Serve fresh
Stir once and serve immediately. You can add ice cubes if desired.
![Sugarcane Juice](images/Sugarcane1.jpg){md}

## 🌿 Tips & Variations
Adjust jaggery based on sweetness preference.
Best consumed fresh for maximum taste and nutrients.
Avoid storing for long, as jaggery-based drinks ferment quickly.
